About Aritro Saha

Aritro Saha (also known as Megh) is a Software Engineer 1 at Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS) specializing in high-throughput data pipelines, cloud infrastructure, native macOS utilities, full-stack web engineering, and applied AI. He graduated with a B.Tech in Electronics and Computer Engineering from Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT Chennai) in 2025 and won Hack4Bengal 3.0, Eastern India's largest hackathon.

His technical stack spans Python, TypeScript, JavaScript, Swift 6.0, React 19, Next.js, FastAPI, Databricks, Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, Firestore), Apple Vision Framework, and Three.js / WebAssembly.
